# History

## Version 0.2.x
Adds `customProperties` (the shared Bitol key/value extension structure) and `relatesTo` on results (linking an observation back to a specific element of the referenced contract), and expands the documentation to match the ODCS/ODPS structure.

OORS v0.2.0 is currently in development.

## Version 0.1.x
The first draft of the Open Observability Results Standard (OORS), a lightweight format for exchanging data quality and data observability results. OORS is championed within the Bitol TSC and defined by [RFC-0018](https://github.com/bitol-io/tsc/blob/main/rfcs/0018-oors.md), authored by Patrick Beitsma, Diego Carvallo, and Jean-Georges Perrin.

OORS v0.1.0 is currently in development. Tracked RFCs are listed in the [`tsc/rfcs/`](https://github.com/bitol-io/tsc/tree/main/rfcs) directory.

## Origins
OORS was created within [Bitol](https://bitol.io), part of the [Linux Foundation AI & Data](https://lfaidata.foundation/), alongside its sibling standards ODCS (Open Data Contract Standard) and ODPS (Open Data Product Standard). It follows the same lightweight, human-readable philosophy, focusing specifically on the exchange of observability and data quality results.
